Azinox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Azinox processes information when you use the Azinox mobile application.

Azinox is a decision-support tool intended to help users identify possible scams, fraud, phishing, impersonation, and social-engineering attempts. Its results are informational and do not guarantee that a message is legitimate or fraudulent.

1. Who operates Azinox

Azinox is operated by an independent developer based in Spain. For privacy questions or requests, contact: YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

2. Information processed by Azinox

Azinox may process the following information:

  • Content submitted for analysis: text, messages, links, or other content that you voluntarily submit. Where image analysis is available, this may also include screenshots or images that you deliberately select.
  • Anonymous account identifier: the application may generate an anonymous user identifier through its authentication provider so that requests can be authenticated and protected against abuse.
  • Analysis results: risk scores, detected categories, warning signs, recommendations, confidence estimates, and other generated results.
  • Technical information: limited diagnostic information such as request status, error type, application version, and request timing may be processed to maintain reliability and security.
  • Usage-limit information: an anonymous identifier and request counters may be processed to enforce rate limits and prevent automated or abusive use.
  • Purchase information: if subscriptions are enabled, Google Play and the subscription management provider may process purchase status, subscription status, product identifiers, renewal information, and an anonymous customer identifier.

3. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Provide scam and fraud analysis requested by the user.
  • Generate explanations and recommended safety actions.
  • Authenticate legitimate application requests.
  • Prevent abuse, excessive usage, and unauthorised access.
  • Maintain application stability and troubleshoot errors.
  • Manage free and premium access where subscriptions are enabled.
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

4. Artificial intelligence processing

Content submitted for analysis may be transmitted securely to Azinox's backend and to an artificial intelligence service provider for the sole purpose of producing the requested analysis.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, bank credentials, identification numbers, medical information, or other information that is not necessary for the analysis.

AI-generated results may be incomplete or incorrect. Users should verify important matters through independently located official channels and should not rely on Azinox as legal, financial, law-enforcement, or professional advice.

5. Local history and storage

Analysis history may be stored locally on your device to provide the history feature. Local information can generally be removed by deleting entries within the application, clearing the application's storage, or uninstalling the application.

Content submitted for analysis is transmitted for processing. Azinox does not intentionally create advertising profiles from submitted content and does not sell submitted messages or analysis results.

6. Service providers

Azinox relies on third-party service providers acting as infrastructure or processing providers. Depending on the features enabled, these may include:

  • Supabase: backend infrastructure, authentication, database, and serverless functions.
  • OpenAI: artificial intelligence processing used to generate structured analysis.
  • Upstash: rate limiting and protection against excessive or abusive requests.
  • Google Play: application distribution, billing, subscription purchases, and related platform services.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status and entitlement management, if premium subscriptions are enabled.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies and may process data in countries outside your place of residence.

7. Data sharing

Azinox does not sell personal information. Information is shared only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the analysis or subscription service.
  • Operate, secure, and maintain the application.
  • Comply with a valid legal obligation.
  • Protect users, the developer, or the service from fraud or abuse.

8. Data retention

Azinox aims to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Local history remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, the application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled. Third-party providers may apply their own limited retention periods for security, billing, or service-operation purposes.

9. Security

Azinox uses reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated backend requests, encrypted network connections, restricted server-side credentials, and request rate limiting.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should not submit information that is unnecessary for analysing the suspicious content.

10. Legal basis for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, information may be processed because:

  • processing is necessary to provide the service requested by you;
  • you voluntarily submit content for analysis;
  • there is a legitimate interest in securing, maintaining, and improving the service;
  • processing is required to comply with a legal obligation.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding personal information associated with you.

Because Azinox currently uses anonymous identifiers, it may not always be possible to associate server-side information with a specific person unless sufficient technical information is provided.

To make a privacy or deletion request, contact YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL.

12. Children's privacy

Azinox is not specifically directed at young children. Users should not submit personal information belonging to a child. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Azinox, contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

13. International processing

The service providers used by Azinox may process information outside Spain or the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate contractual or legal safeguards are used by those providers for international transfers.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Azinox introduces new features, changes service providers, or must comply with new legal or platform requirements. The latest version will remain available on this page, and its effective date will be updated.
